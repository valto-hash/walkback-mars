04 · Evidence and uncertainty

Show what is real. Show what is estimated.

Embedded mission data

698

NASA/JPL Mars 2020 Perseverance waypoint records spanning Sol 13–2022.

Optional: USGS / MRO CTX Jezero DEM (~20 m native) has a processing and import workflow, but is not bundled or loaded by default.

Terrain and slope
Approx. 215 m solver grid; default terrain is procedural outside the rover corridor. Optional CTX DEM is sampled to this same coarse grid, too coarse for foot-scale hazards.
Walking cost
Minetti-inspired slope-cost proxy; the 0.38-g scaling is heuristic. Terrain multipliers and uncertainty are adjustable assumptions, not validated EVA physiology.
THEMIS / HiRISE importers
THEMIS accepts qualitative 8-bit visual mosaics; HiRISE accepts separate local elevation patches. No orbital raster is bundled or preloaded.
Environmental limits
No soil-strength, solar-energy or location-specific radiation-dose model.
Communications
Editable single-hop free-space link-budget estimate plus geometric line of sight; not a relay-network design.

06 · Roadmap and conclusion

From an honest prototype to a stronger mission model.

Use real orbital rasters
Download and projection-check CTX, THEMIS and HiRISE source products; load a verified CTX grid and local HiRISE patches.
Multi-mission science
Integrate validated CRISM, geology, soil, illumination and environmental products. Keep visual mosaics separate from quantitative measurements.
Model validation
Validate walking-cost assumptions for 0.38 g and suited EVA with domain experts.
Operational extensions
Validated shelter attenuation, solar-weather input, RF network/relay design and emergency caches.
