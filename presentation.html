03 · The core idea

Two envelopes. Two different questions.

BLUE · Walk-back

How far can an explorer travel and still return on the remaining consumables?

Inputs: consumable time, flat-ground speed, safety margin.

GREEN · Shelter reach

From which locations can the selected shelter be reached within the configured warning interval?

Input: configurable warning window.

04 · Data and uncertainty

Show the evidence. Show the limits.

Embedded mission data

698

NASA/JPL Mars 2020 Perseverance waypoint records, Sol 13–2022, with position, elevation and odometer fields.

Terrain
Procedural beyond the rover corridor; approx. 200–215 m grid.
Walking model
Tobler-inspired; not adjusted for 0.38 g or suit physiology.
Solar and radiation
No live solar forecast or local radiation-dose simulation.
Interpretation
Exploratory prototype, not a certified EVA safety system.
